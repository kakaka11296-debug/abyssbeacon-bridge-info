Privacy Policy

AbyssBeacon Bridge is a personal utility used by its owner to connect AbyssBeacon, Google Drive, and a Paperspace environment.

Google user data

Google Drive access is used only for the owner's private workflow, such as transferring small queue, bridge, and status files.

The application does not sell Google user data. It does not use Google user data for advertising. It does not share Google user data with third parties except when necessary to operate the owner's own workflow.

Data retention

Bridge and queue files may be retained in the owner's own Google Drive until the owner deletes them.

Access control

This application is intended for private personal use by the owner.
